# Dope Wars

Welcome to the official documentation of **Dope Wars**, a real-money skill game built with [Dojo Engine](https://dojoengine.org) on [Starknet](https://starknet.io).

## About

Dope Wars is an onchain adaptation of the classic Drug Wars game. Pay an entry fee, build your hustler, navigate encounters through fight, flee, or finesse decisions, and compete for MTT-style payouts. The drug trading is the economic engine. The encounters are the game.

## Core mechanics

- **Entry fee**: Pay USD to enter a ranked game. 70% buys and burns PAPER, 30% to developer
- **Build your hustler**: 4 equipment slots (Weapon, Clothes, Feet, Transport) shape your playstyle
- **15-21 turns**: Navigate 6 NYC neighborhoods, trading drugs and encountering Cops and Gangs
- **Fight, Run, or Pay**: Every encounter is a three-way skill decision driven by your build
- **Season competition**: 7 randomized dimensions create 2,187 possible season configurations
