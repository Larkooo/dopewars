# Seasons

Every season randomizes 7 dimensions, creating **2,187 possible configurations**. Like a new DFS slate every week, the optimal strategy changes and the meta resets.

## Season settings

Each dimension has 3 variants, randomly selected via VRF at season creation.

| Dimension | Variant 1 | Variant 2 | Variant 3 |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| CashMode | Broke (420) | Average (1,000) | Rich (2,600) |
| HealthMode | Junkie (70) | Hustler (90) | Streetboss (110) |
| TurnsMode | OnSpeed (15) | OnWeed (18) | OnMush (21) |
| EncountersMode | Chill (-2 stats) | NoJokes (base) | UltraViolence (+2/+3) |
| EncountersOddsMode | Easy (/20) | Normal (/16) | Hard (/12) |
| DrugsMode | Cheap | Normal | Expensive |
| WantedMode | KoolAndTheGang | ThugLife | MostWanted |

## How settings change optimal play

- **Broke + UltraViolence** — Fighter builds dominate. You can't afford to lose drugs or cash to Pay. Every encounter is a must-win.
- **Rich + Chill** — Trade-heavy Finesse plays are viable. Encounters are manageable, starting capital lets you invest in drugs immediately.
- **OnSpeed + Hard** — Reputation races matter more. With only 15 turns and fast encounter scaling, commit to a build archetype early.
- **OnMush + KoolAndTheGang** — Runner builds thrive. 21 turns with low encounter odds means more trading turns. High Transport pays off.

## Fixed per-season config

These values are constant across all seasons:

| Parameter | Value | Description |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| max_wanted_shopping | 5 | Wanted threshold to enter pawnshop |
| max_rounds | 3 | Max rounds when running from encounter |
| rep_drug_step | 20 | Reputation needed per drug level |
| rep_buy_item | 3 | Rep earned per equipment purchase |
| rep_carry_drugs | 2 | Rep earned per turn carrying > 5 drugs |
| rep_hospitalized | 4 | Rep earned when hospitalized |
| rep_jailed | 6 | Rep earned when jailed |
