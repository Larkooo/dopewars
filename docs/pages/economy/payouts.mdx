# Rewards

Dope Wars uses a **dynamic per-game reward system**. PAPER is issued immediately at the end of each round based on individual performance. There is no pooled jackpot or tournament payout.

## Reward function

The reward is computed per game based on:

| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Player performance** | Final cash at end of round. Higher cash = higher reward. |
| **Player item level** | Higher item level raises encounter difficulty, which increases the reward multiplier. Better gear = harder game = more PAPER. |
| **PAPER token dynamics** | Supply and market conditions factor into the computation. |

## Item-level scaling

This is the key economic mechanic. Your equipment choices don't just affect gameplay, they affect your reward ceiling.

- **Low item level** — Easier encounters, lower PAPER rewards per game.
- **High item level** — Harder encounters, higher PAPER rewards per game.

This creates a natural progression loop: earn PAPER, upgrade gear, face harder challenges, earn more PAPER. Players self-select their difficulty by choosing their build.

:::tip
**The tradeoff is real.** A Fighter with Weapon Lv3 faces tougher encounters but earns more PAPER per win. A Runner with Feet Lv3 faces the same difficulty scaling but earns rewards through trade volume rather than combat. The reward function accounts for overall item level, not specific slot allocation.
:::

## Entry fee distribution

Every USD entry fee is split:

| Allocation | Percentage | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Buy-back and burn | 70% | Purchases PAPER from the AMM and burns it. Reduces supply. |
| Developer | 30% | Platform revenue and continued development. |

## Key properties

- **Not zero-sum.** Your reward depends on your individual performance, not on how other players did.
- **Immediate.** No season-end processing, no claim transactions. PAPER arrives in your wallet at round end.
- **Deflationary.** 70% of all entry fees permanently remove PAPER from circulation. The more games played, the more PAPER is burned.
- **Self-balancing.** Item-level difficulty scaling means the reward function naturally adjusts. Players who push into harder territory earn more, but only if they can perform.
