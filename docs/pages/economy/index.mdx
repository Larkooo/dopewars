# Economy

Dope Wars uses a **USD entry / PAPER reward** economy. Players pay USD to enter, earn PAPER based on individual performance, and 70% of all entry fees buy and burn PAPER from the market.

The more people play, the more PAPER is bought and burned. This creates constant deflationary pressure on the token supply.

## Reward model

Rewards are **per-game, immediate, and individual**. No jackpot, no waiting for a season to end.

The reward function is dynamic, factoring in:
- **Player performance** — Final cash at end of round
- **Player item level** — Higher item level = harder difficulty = higher reward ceiling
- **PAPER token dynamics** — Supply and market conditions

:::note
**Item-level scaling is the key mechanic.** Equipping better gear raises your difficulty, which raises your potential PAPER reward. This creates a natural progression: better gear = harder game = more PAPER if you can handle it.
:::
