# FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Dope Wars.

## Gameplay

### What is Dope Wars?

Dope Wars is a real-money skill game built onchain with Dojo Engine on Starknet. It's an adaptation of the classic Drug Wars game where you build a hustler, trade drugs across NYC neighborhoods, and navigate encounters with Cops and Gangs. Your build decisions and encounter choices determine your payout.

### How do I win?

End the game with the most cash possible. Your final cash determines your PAPER reward, issued immediately at round end. Higher performance and higher item level = more PAPER earned.

### What are the three build archetypes?

- **Fighter** — Max Weapon + Clothes. Tank encounters for combat payouts. High risk, high reward.
- **Runner** — Max Feet + Transport. Evade encounters, carry more drugs. Consistent, lower ceiling.
- **Finesse** — Balanced build. Adapt per encounter. Highest skill ceiling.

See [The Build](/game-rules/builds) for detailed loadouts and strategy.

### How do encounters work?

When you travel, you may encounter Cops or Gangs based on your wanted level. You choose to **Fight** (use Attack/Defense), **Run** (use Speed), or **Pay** (lose a percentage of drugs or cash). Your build determines which choice is optimal. See [Encounters](/game-rules/encounters).

### What is reputation?

Reputation (0-100) gates drug access and scales encounter difficulty. Higher rep = better drugs but harder enemies. It's the core tension of the game. See [Reputation](/game-rules/reputation).

### How long is a game?

15-21 turns depending on the season's TurnsMode (OnSpeed = 15, OnWeed = 18, OnMush = 21). Each turn involves traveling, trading, and potentially handling an encounter.

## Economy & token

### How do I enter a game?

Pay a USD entry fee to enter a Ranked game. 70% of the fee buys PAPER from the AMM and burns it. 30% goes to the developer.

### What is the $PAPER token?

$PAPER is the reward token of Dope Wars. Players earn PAPER based on their in-game performance. Entry fees are paid in USD, not PAPER. 70% of all entry fees buy and burn PAPER, creating deflationary pressure.

### How do rewards work?

PAPER is issued immediately at the end of each round based on a dynamic reward function. Your reward depends on your performance (final cash) and your item level (higher item level = harder game = more PAPER). Rewards are individual, not pooled. See [Rewards](/economy/payouts).

### What are seasons?

Seasons define the game parameters: 7 randomized dimensions (2,187 possible configs) that change starting cash, health, turns, encounter difficulty, drug pricing, and wanted escalation. Rewards are per-game within a season, not paid out when the season ends. See [Seasons](/economy/seasons).

## Technical

### Is the game provably fair?

Yes. All game logic runs onchain on Starknet. Randomness comes from Cartridge VRF (verifiable random function). Every encounter outcome, payout calculation, and season configuration is verifiable. See [Contracts](/contracts).

### What wallets are supported?

Cartridge Controller, ArgentX, Braavos, and MetaMask (via Starknet bridge).

### Can I play for free?

Yes. **Noob mode** is free with no payouts. You can also use a Guest identity (8 free IDs per season) instead of an NFT.

### Where is the game available?

Dope Wars is available worldwide except in certain US states (AZ, AR, CT, DE, LA, MT, SC, SD, TN) where skill-based real-money gaming regulations apply.
