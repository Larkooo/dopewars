# Game Rules

Dope Wars is a real-money skill game. Players land in a fictional NYC, build their hustler through equipment upgrades, and navigate encounters with Cops and Gangs. Your build decisions and encounter choices determine your payout.

## Win conditions

End the game with the **most cash** possible. Your final cash determines your PAPER reward through a dynamic reward function. Higher performance = more PAPER, issued immediately at round end. Your reward also scales with your item level: better gear = harder game = bigger potential payout.

## Lose conditions

- **Death** — Health reaches 0 during an encounter. Game over immediately.
- **Low performance** — Ending with less cash than you started means minimal or no PAPER reward.
- **Mismanaged wanted** — Triggering encounters your build can't handle.
- **Over-invested in equipment** — No capital left to trade drugs.
