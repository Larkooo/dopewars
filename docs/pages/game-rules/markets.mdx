# Markets

Drug trading is the economic engine of Dope Wars. Markets fund your build and generate the cash that becomes your final score.

## How markets work

Each location has a **constant-product market** (Uniswap-like) for drugs. Your trades move prices, creating information asymmetry across locations.

- **8 drugs** in ascending price tiers
- You carry only **1 drug type** at a time
- Carry capacity = Transport stat vs (drug weight x quantity)
- Price formula: `price = base + (tick x step)`, tick range 0-63
- Markets randomly vary at the end of each turn

## Drug access

Drug access is gated by `drug_level`, which advances with reputation:

`drug_level = min(reputation / 20, 4)`

You can only trade drugs within a sliding window of 4:

| Level | Rep needed | Drugs available |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| 0 | 0 | Ludes, Speed, Weed, Shrooms |
| 1 | 20 | Speed, Weed, Shrooms, Acid |
| 2 | 40 | Weed, Shrooms, Acid, Ketamine |
| 3 | 60 | Shrooms, Acid, Ketamine, Heroin |
| 4 | 80 | Acid, Ketamine, Heroin, Cocaine |

Higher-tier drugs have higher prices and margins but weigh more, requiring Transport investment.
