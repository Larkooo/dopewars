# Encounters

Encounters are where your build matters. Every encounter is a meaningful three-way decision: Fight, Run, or Pay.

## Encounter level

Encounters scale with reputation. Higher reputation means harder enemies.

`level = reputation / divisor + 1` (capped at 6)

| EncountersOddsMode | Divisor | Level at rep 60 | Level at rep 100 |
|--------------------|---------|-----------------|------------------|
| Easy | 20 | 4 | 6 |
| Normal | 16 | 4 | 6 |
| Hard | 12 | 6 | 6 |

## Encounter stats

Stats scale with level: `stat = base + (level x step)`

### NoJokes baseline

| Type | HP Base/Step | ATK Base/Step | DEF Base/Step | SPD Base/Step |
|------|-------------|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| Cops | 12 / 8 | 14 / 8 | 16 / 9 | 6 / 8 |
| Gang | 1 / 11 | 5 / 11 | 7 / 8 | 2 / 8 |

### Difficulty adjustments

| Mode | Cops | Gang |
|------|------|------|
| Chill | All stats -2 | Base unchanged, step -2 |
| NoJokes | Baseline | Baseline |
| UltraViolence | All stats +2 | All stats +3 |

## Fight

Choose Fight when your Attack exceeds their effective HP.

- Speed race determines initiative (who attacks first each round).
- Attack = `player_attack +/- 20%` random variance.
- Damage shielded = `attack x (defender_defense / 100)`.
- Net damage = attack - shielded.
- Continues until one side dies (no round limit).

**Victory payout:** `(encounter_level x 3 + (turn / 5)^2) x 1,000` cash.

**Victory rep:** +encounter_level x 3.

:::tip
**When to fight:** Fighter builds with Weapon Lv3 can clear most encounters profitably. The payout scales with level and turn, so late-game fights at high reputation are the biggest cash injections in the game.
:::

## Run

Choose Run when Speed is your edge and you're carrying valuable inventory.

- Speed race: `random(0, player_speed)` vs `random(0, encounter_speed)`. Up to **3 rounds**.
- Each failed round: encounter attacks at attack/5 damage, lose max(1% drugs, 2 units).
- **Escaped:** Rep gain = level x 2.
- **Caught** (after 3 rounds): Redirected to random location.
  - Cops: **Jailed** (lose 2 turns). Rep +6, -(level x 2).
  - Gang: **Hospitalized** (lose 1 turn). Rep +4, -(level x 2).

:::tip
**When to run:** Runner builds with Feet Lv3 have a dominant speed advantage. Running preserves turns and inventory, which compounds over the session.
:::

## Pay

Choose Pay as a calculated loss when the encounter outclasses your build.

- **Cops:** Confiscate `demand_pct`% of your drugs.
- **Gang:** Take `demand_pct`% of your cash + 1 HP damage (non-lethal).
- **Rep loss:** level x 5.

### demand_pct distribution

| Roll (0-99) | < 1 | 1-9 | 10-19 | 20-49 | 50-99 |
|-------------|-----|-----|-------|-------|-------|
| demand_pct | 69% | 50% | 40% | 30% | 20% |

:::tip
**When to pay:** Paying 20% of your drugs (50% chance) is better than dying. Dead hustlers don't score. Finesse players pay strategically to preserve resources for fights they can win.
:::

## Outcomes

| Outcome | Condition | Effect |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Died | Health reaches 0 | Game over |
| Paid | Chose Pay | Lose drugs (Cops) or cash (Gang) |
| Escaped | Won Run speed check | Continue to destination |
| Victorious | Won Fight | Cash payout + reputation |
| Jailed | Failed Run vs Cops | Lose 2 turns, random location |
| Hospitalized | Failed Run vs Gang | Lose 1 turn, random location |
