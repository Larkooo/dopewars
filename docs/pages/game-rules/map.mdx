## Wanted level

Each location tracks its own **wanted level** (3-bit value, range 0-7). Wanted determines encounter probability.

### Encounter probability

`encounter_risk = (wanted x 15) - risk_modifier`

| Wanted | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|--------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw risk | 0% | 15% | 30% | 45% | 60% | 75% | 90% | 105% |

Risk is reduced by the season's risk modifier (see below).

### Wanted mode

The season's WantedMode determines how fast wanted escalates.

| Mode | Risk modifier | Travel-back | Leave-with-drugs |
|------|--------------|-------------|------------------|
| KoolAndTheGang | -20 | +2 | +5 |
| ThugLife | -15 | +3 | +5 |
| MostWanted | -10 | +4 | +6 |

:::note
**Warrior mode** always triggers encounters regardless of wanted level. Wanted still accumulates but doesn't affect encounter probability.
:::

## Strategy

High-wanted locations have better market opportunities (your trades have been moving prices there) but higher encounter risk. Low-wanted locations are safe but margins are thinner.

- **Fighters** want encounters. Travel aggressively.
- **Runners** avoid encounters. Rotate locations to keep wanted low.
- **Finesse** players read the situation. Sometimes you run toward danger, sometimes away.
