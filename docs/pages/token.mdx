# PAPER Token

**PAPER** is the reward token of Dope Wars. Players earn PAPER through gameplay performance. PAPER is burned to acquire permanent gear through the VRGDA item auction. Entry fees are paid in USD.

## Two burn sources

PAPER has two distinct burn mechanisms:

**Entry fee burn:** Every USD entry fee automatically purchases PAPER from the AMM and burns it. The more games played, the more PAPER is removed from circulation.

**VRGDA item burn:** Players choose to burn PAPER to permanently acquire equipment items through the Variable Rate Gradual Dutch Auction. This connects the token economy directly to the build system: the better your permanent gear, the more PAPER you burned to get it.

## Reward mechanics

PAPER rewards are computed dynamically per game based on:

- **Performance** — Your final cash when the game ends
- **Item level** — Higher total item level increases encounter difficulty, which increases the reward multiplier
- **Token dynamics** — Supply and market conditions factor into the computation

Rewards are **immediate**. PAPER is issued at round end with no claim step and no waiting period.

:::note
**Item-level scaling is the key mechanic.** Equipping better gear raises your difficulty, which raises your potential PAPER reward. This creates a natural progression loop: earn PAPER, buy permanent gear through VRGDA, face harder challenges, earn more PAPER.
:::

## VRGDA Item Auction

The Variable Rate Gradual Dutch Auction is how players acquire permanent equipment.

- **Pricing:** Starts at a target price and decays over time. If items are purchased faster than the target rate, the price rises. If demand is low, the price falls.
- **Items:** All 9 equipment slots (Weapon, Clothes, Feet, Vehicle, Waist, Hand, Neck, Ring, Drug) are available through the auction.
- **Permanence:** VRGDA items persist across sessions. They define your baseline build before any in-game Pawnshop upgrades.
- **Burns PAPER:** Every VRGDA purchase removes PAPER from circulation permanently.
